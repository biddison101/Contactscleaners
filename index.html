How it works

A cleanup, step by step.

Scroll to see how Contacts Cleaners tidies your address book.

01Scan

See your Contact Health at a glance.

The Health Score shows how tidy your address book is, and Suggestions point out what needs attention. Fix it, leave it, or come back later.

02Backup first

A backup comes first. Every time.

Before any change, Contacts Cleaners saves a backup and records it in History. Undo a merge or roll back to any point.

03Merge

Merge duplicates you can trust.

Contacts are only grouped when they share a real phone number or email, so different people with the same name are never merged. Preview the result and choose what to keep.

04Fix

Spot what’s missing.

Find contacts missing a phone, email, or name, and tap to fix them.

05Swipe to Clean

Swipe through the old ones.

Review old and empty contacts one at a time and keep the ones that matter. Removing them is part of Pro.

06Auto-monitor

Stays clean on its own.

With Pro, turn on Monitoring Alerts and get notified when new duplicates appear.

Suggestions

Private, on your iPhone

FAQ

Good questions.

Can it merge two different people by mistake?+

No. Contacts are only grouped when they share a real phone number or email, so different people with the same name are never merged.

What if I change my mind?+

A backup is made before every change, and every merge, delete and Auto-Clean run can be undone from History.

Where are my backups saved?+

Backups are saved to the Files app on your iPhone, where you can keep or share them.

Are my contacts uploaded anywhere?+

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. Contacts Cleaners has no servers, no account and no tracking.

Does it work with iCloud and Google contacts?+

Yes. It cleans contacts from iCloud, Google, Exchange and other accounts on your iPhone, and changes sync back automatically.

Is there a subscription?+

No. Pro is a one-time purchase, and you can restore it anytime on your devices.
